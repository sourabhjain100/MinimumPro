How to Play
Game Rules
The Objective

Be the player with the lowest score at the end of each round. The game typically ends when a player reaches a pre-set point limit (e.g., 200 points), or after a specific number of rounds.

Card Values
  • Joker: 0 Point (Most precious card)
  • Ace: 1 Point
  • 2–10: Face Value
  • Jack: 11 Points
  • Queen: 12 Points
  • King: 13 Points
Hand Setup

Deal: Each player is dealt 5 cards.

The Deck: The remaining cards are placed face-down (the draw pile).

The Open Card: One card is flipped face-up next to the deck to start the discard pile.

The Turn

On your turn, you must perform two actions:

Discard: Throw at least one card onto the open pile.

  • Single: Any one card.
  • Sets: Multiple cards of the same rank (e.g., a pair of Queens, three 8s).
  • Sequences: A run of 3 or more cards of the same suit (e.g., 4, 5, and 6 of Spades).

Pick Up: Draw one card to replace what you threw. You can take either:

  • The top card of the face-down deck.
  • The exact card(s) the player immediately before you just threw.
Calling "Minimum"

Requirement: Your total hand value must be 9 points or less.

When: You call it at the start of your turn.

Action: When calling Minimum, you do not draw or discard. You simply announce "Minimum" and show your cards.

The Showdown: All other players reveal their hands to compare totals.

Scoring Logic
  • Successful Call: If the caller has the lowest score, they get -20 points.
  • Caught (Failed Call): If someone else has a score equal to or lower than the caller, the caller gets 50 points, and the "true winner" gets 0 points.
  • Standard Score: All other players receive points equal to the total value of the cards in their hand.
How to use the app
1. Player Management
  • Add Player: Type a name and tap Add Player.
  • Re-Add: Frequent players appear in the "Re-add" list. Tap them to quickly bring them into a new game session.
  • Remove: Tap Remove on a player's card to skip them for the current round.
2. Digital Scorekeeping
  • The Calculator: Tap Set to open the card tray. Tap the cards a player is holding (e.g., A, 2, 5) and the app will calculate the total for you.
  • Quick Buttons: Use the -20, 0, and 50 buttons for the winners and losers of the "Minimum" call.
  • Manual Entry: You can also type scores directly into the input box next to the player's name.
3. History & Persistence
  • Auto-Save: Your game progress is saved to Local Storage. You can refresh the page or close the browser without losing your data.
  • Edit Rounds: Double tap any row in the history table to reload that round. Fix the scores and tap Update to correct the standings.
  • Export: Tap Image to save a high-quality JPG of the final results to share with the group.
4. Visual Indicators
  • 👑 Winner: The player with the lowest total is highlighted in Gold in the footer.
  • 🚫 Loser: The player with the highest total is highlighted in Red in the footer.
